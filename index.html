Northwood Space Explained: Portal, the Phased-Array Ground Station Built for Scale

Launching got cheap, but talking to satellites did not. This is how Northwood Space wants to rebuild the Earth end of every mission.

Based in El Segundo, California, Northwood Space makes the ground hardware that links spacecraft to people. Its Portal system is a digital phased array intended for volume production and installation within days, where dish-based sites often need years. New to the company? Start with the Northwood Space resource.

Why ground segments are the choke point

Rockets are cheaper and constellations keep growing, yet the dishes that uplink commands and downlink data are largely a 1960s design. Booking ground access can now outlast the build and launch of the satellite. Northwood sells itself as an end-to-end provider, covering concept, deployment and live data delivery.

Inside Portal

A parabolic dish has to swing toward a single satellite. Portal is a flat all-digital array that steers beams electronically, so one site can follow multiple objects at the same time.

  • Many beams: around ten concurrent satellite connections.
  • Bandwidth: more than 1 Gbps on each beam.
  • Any orbit: low, medium or geostationary.
  • Quick build: flat panels meant for fast manufacturing and on-site setup within days.

Timeline

  • Oct 2023: Mendler, Cleverly and Luthra found the company.
  • Feb 2024: $6.3M seed announced.
  • Mid-2024: prototype “Frankie” links with Planet Labs satellites from Maddock, North Dakota, over five two-way passes in S- and X-band.
  • 2025: first production phased-array station is designed, built and on orbit within four months, running live with Planet.
  • Jan 2026: $100M Series B and a $49.8M Space Force contract.

People

Bridgit Mendler (CEO) was a Disney Channel actress and singer before earning MIT and Harvard degrees and moving into space. Griffin Cleverly is CTO and Shaurya Luthra runs software, both formerly Lockheed Martin engineers. The concept grew in the COVID-19 pandemic, when Mendler and Cleverly assembled antennas from hardware-store parts; the name comes from a New Hampshire lake.

Likely customers

  • Constellation operators in Earth observation and communications that are short on ground capacity.
  • Defence programmes updating satellite command and control.
  • New missions that want flexible access without multi-year procurement.

Quick answers

What is Northwood Space’s business?It designs and runs phased-array ground stations so satellite operators can command spacecraft and download data.
What exactly is Portal?Portal is the company’s all-digital phased-array station, built to hold several satellite contacts at once across different orbits.
Who started Northwood Space?Bridgit Mendler, Griffin Cleverly and Shaurya Luthra, in 2023.
Does the company make satellites or launch vehicles?No. Its focus is ground infrastructure: antennas, software and network services.
